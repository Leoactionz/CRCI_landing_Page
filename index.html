Home/About CRCi

A reading mentor for every child who needs one.

CRCi Hub is a literacy and mentorship program for learners aged 5 to 14 across Africa. We pair children with vetted mentors for one-on-one reading, and we show guardians every step.

Enroll your wardEnroll your ward

Early readers need attention a busy classroom can't always give.

Phonics and fluency hurdles are easiest to clear one-on-one. CRCi Hub gives every learner a mentor's full attention for a focused weekly session.

A typical classroom

One teacher, a full room

Children who are stuck on sounds or slow to decode can go unnoticed for a whole term.

A CRCi session

One mentor, one learner

Thirty minutes a week of reading aloud together, with feedback on every word that trips them up.

Schools, parents and mentors, linked around each child.

Nobody hosts a session until they've cleared every check.

Every CRCi mentor passes the same three checks before they meet a learner.

Background check

Identity and background screening for every applicant.

Credential verification

We confirm each mentor's education credentials.

15+ hours of training

Specialised child literacy training before the first session.

Ready to mentor

Matched to learners by schedule, language and pace.

Home/How it works

Four steps from first test to first chapter book.

Scroll through what happens after you enroll, from a 10-minute baseline test to the moment you sign off your child's next book.

01

Diagnostic assessment

A 10-minute baseline reading test identifies your child's reading stage and vocabulary needs.

  • Done online, at home
  • Sets the starting level
  • Results shared with you
02

Mentor alignment

We match your child with a certified literacy coach based on schedule, language and learning pace.

  • Vetted, trained mentors
  • A time that suits your family
  • A mentor who speaks your child's language
03

Guided reading sessions

Weekly 30-minute live sessions where mentor and child read together and work through difficult words.

  • Supervised digital rooms
  • Real-time pronunciation feedback
  • Every session recorded
04

Guardian verification

You watch session recordings, read mentor notes and sign off on book milestones in the portal.

  • Full audit trail
  • Sign off from your phone
  • Weekly progress summary
